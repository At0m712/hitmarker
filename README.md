### HitMarker Enhanced (HME) is a HitMarker indicator when you hit a mob or player with a projectile (Arrow, Egg, Trident, etc...).


<br>

![Video demonstration of the mod]()

<br> 

![Configurations Logo](https://i.imgur.com/vZ4ypIX.png)

<br>

HME come with 3 different HitMarker style and allows you to toggle the hit sound, Use the commands below to customize your experience!

![HitMarker styles](https://i.imgur.com/mKRvC3Q.png)

+ ` /HitMarker Style 1 `
+ ` /HitMarker Style 2 `
+ ` /HitMarker Style 3 `
+ ` /HitMarker Sound true/false`
